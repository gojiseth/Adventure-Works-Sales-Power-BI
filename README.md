Adventure Works Sales Power BI Dashboard

## Project Overview
Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Adventure Works 2017 sales data. Focus on revenue performance, profit margins, order trends, and product performance to drive business decisions.

## Key Metrics
- **Total Sales**: $9.19M
- **Total Orders**: 45K
- **Total Profit**: $3.50M
- **Total Cost**: $1.89M
- **Profit Margin**: 42.3%

## Key Insights
1. **Strong Profitability**: 42.3% profit margin shows business is profitable with good cost control
2. **January Spike**: January had massive sales spike of >$5.5M - likely bulk/corporate order
3. **Seasonality**: Daily revenue fluctuates with peaks in Jan and smaller spikes through year
4. **Top Products**: Mountain-200 is most profitable product at $256,765.34 profit

## Recommendations
1. Maintain current pricing strategy but negotiate better supplier rates to increase margin above 45%
2. Investigate January's spike and create yearly campaign to replicate Q1 2023
3. Run clearance discounts to clear bottom 5 products with <2,720 orders each
4. Expand in underperforming countries - US dominates, others need local partnerships

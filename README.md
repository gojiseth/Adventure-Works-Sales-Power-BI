# Adventure Works Sales Dashboard - H1 2017

Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Adventure Works sales performance Jan 2017 to Jun 2017. Built for Data Analysis course portfolio.



**Name:** Goji Seth Gabe  
**Course:** Data Analysis  
**Period:** 1 Jan 2017 - 30 Jun 2017  
**Date:** 5 June 2026

## Key KPIs
- **Total Sales:** $489.31K
- **Total Profit:** $208.60K  
- **Profit Margin:** 42.6%
- **Total Orders:** 1K orders
- **Total Quantity:** 3K units
- **Total Cost:** $280.71K

## Insights & Recommendations

### 1. KPIs - Overall Business Performance
**Insight:** H1 2017 is profitable with strong cost control. Margin at 42.6% = $208.6K / $489.31K  
**Recommendation:** Maintain current pricing. Target 45%+ margin by negotiating 2-3% supplier discount. At 42.6%, every $10K cost cut = $10K extra profit.

### 2. Monthly Sales Distribution
**Insight:** Jan spike at $360,975.21 = 74% of H1 revenue. Feb-May flat $19K-26K. June recovers to $79K. Not seasonal - one bulk/corporate order in Jan.  
**Recommendation:** Identify Jan customer in Sales table. Launch "H2 Bulk Order Campaign" in Aug-Sep targeting B2B buyers. Goal: replicate 30% of Jan revenue in Q3.

### 3. Daily Revenue Distribution
**Insight:** Peak days: Saturday $65,209, Monday $33,976, Sunday $44,765. Dead days: Tuesday $14,342, Wednesday $8,389. 70% revenue from Fri-Sun-Mon.  
**Recommendation:** Push email/SMS promos on Tue/Wed with "Midweek Deal". Stock inventory for Sat/Mon. Analyze if Jan bulk order was placed on Monday.

### 4. Bottom 5 Products by Total Orders
**Insight:** Mountain 500 SL, Mountain 500 BL, Mountain 300 SL, Mountain 300 BL, Touring 1000 SL all at 22-26 orders. <3% of 1K total orders. All slow movers.  
**Recommendation:** Discontinue all 5 SKUs immediately. Run 25% clearance sale to liquidate. Reallocate warehouse space + marketing budget to Mountain-200 series.

### 5. Country by Revenue, Orders & Profit
**Insight:** US $145,103 revenue, 523 orders, $83,064 profit → 52% of orders, $277/order. Australia $149,999 revenue, 296 orders, $58,812 profit → $506/order, highest value. UK, Canada, France, Germany stable but small.  
**Recommendation:** Defend US with loyalty program to protect 52% share. Push Mountain-200 Silver 38 in Australia - highest rev/order + highest profit SKU match. Test local pricing in UK/Canada to lift $/order.

### 6. Most Profitable Product
**Insight:** Mountain-200 Silver, 38 = $15,257.02 profit. Highest profit despite not being highest volume.  
**Recommendation:** Shift 60% production capacity to Mountain-200 Silver 38. Train sales team to upsell this SKU. Use it as "premium" bike vs high-volume Mountain-500.

### 7. Slicer Interactivity
**Insight:** Date slicer 1/1/2017-6/30/2017 + Country slicer filter entire dashboard. Hover tooltips give exact country/month/day data for instant comparison.  
**Recommendation:** Use slicers in monthly ops review. Standard view: "All Countries, All Dates" → Drill: "Australia only, June only" to check if H2 actions worked.

## Conclusion & H2 Action Plan
Adventure Works H1 2017 is profitable at 42.6% margin but faces 3 critical risks: 
1. 74% revenue from single Jan bulk order
2. 5 dead SKUs tying up stock  
3. US volume vs Australia value imbalance

**H2 Target:** $600K revenue with same 42%+ margin  
**Actions:** Replicate Jan deal, kill dead SKUs, push Mountain-200 Silver 38 in Australia, fix Tue/Wed sales dip.
